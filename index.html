01Commercial contracting

Start with the business model.

NewsCorp / NYP Holdings · Legal Clerk · 2025–2026
Experience basis

CV-supported experience; adtech and multiple revenue streams also described by Donny. AI use across this area confirmed by Donny; sequence below is a public reconstruction.

Problem
Commercial agreements must reflect how a business creates value, uses data, and delivers its obligations.
Approach
Map the business objective and revenue structure; identify the relevant services, rights, and dependencies; then work through ownership, data use, indemnity, and the allocation of risk.
AI role
AI-assisted issue organization, comparison of alternatives, and iterative drafting. I use the exchange to surface questions and test options; the commercial objective and final position remain mine to assess.

Prompting & review sequence

Paraphrased intent · reconstructed process · no exact prompts

  1. Frame the transaction

    Prompting intent. Organize the business objective, revenue model, obligations, and open questions.

    My analysis & follow-through. I establish the objective and distinguish known facts from assumptions before considering language.

  2. Compare positions

    Prompting intent. Set out alternative ways to allocate a selected risk and explain the tradeoffs.

    My analysis & follow-through. I compare the options with the business need and identify what needs stakeholder or supervising counsel input.

  3. Challenge the draft

    Prompting intent. Test an option against a competing interpretation or an operational failure scenario.

    My analysis & follow-through. I reject unsupported assumptions and decide whether the language resolves the actual issue.

  4. Incorporate and reconcile

    Prompting intent. Help organize the selected reasoning and flag inconsistent terms.

    My analysis & follow-through. I revise the draft and check the complete agreement, rather than accepting an isolated clause.

Human judgment
Decide which risks matter commercially, which questions require business input, and when a position needs supervising counsel’s review.
Verification
Vetting in this reconstructed approach: reconcile defined terms and cross-references; compare obligations with the agreed scope; confirm business assumptions with the responsible stakeholders.

Scope & privacy. Commercial agreements spanning licensing, marketing, sponsorship, and master services agreements / statements of work. No actual agreement or negotiating position is reproduced.

02AI / ML agreements

Separate the rights before allocating the risk.

NewsCorp / NYP Holdings · AI contracting
Experience basis

CV-supported experience; workflow generalized. AI use across this area confirmed by Donny; sequence below is a public reconstruction.

Problem
An AI arrangement can blur distinct questions about inputs, training, permitted uses, outputs, and ownership.
Approach
Separate each category of data and intellectual property; identify the intended use; distinguish permission to operate a service from permission to train or improve a model; map the corresponding contractual controls.
AI role
AI-assisted decomposition of rights, comparison of contractual treatments, and testing for gaps. I keep the distinction between model behavior, technical assumptions, and enforceable contractual language explicit.

Prompting & review sequence

Paraphrased intent · reconstructed process · no exact prompts

  1. Separate the categories

    Prompting intent. Distinguish inputs, training uses, service uses, outputs, and ownership questions.

    My analysis & follow-through. I check that the categories fit the arrangement and identify unanswered technical questions.

  2. Trace the permissions

    Prompting intent. Map each proposed use to the right or restriction it would require.

    My analysis & follow-through. I distinguish contractual permission from an assumption about how a system operates.

  3. Test the edge cases

    Prompting intent. Explore how a change in data use or model improvement could affect the rights analysis.

    My analysis & follow-through. I decide which scenarios are material and which require clarification rather than speculative drafting.

  4. Bring the analysis into the agreement

    Prompting intent. Compare ways to express the chosen allocation of rights.

    My analysis & follow-through. I reconcile the language with definitions, restrictions, and the overall commercial structure.

Human judgment
Interpret the rights actually granted, evaluate the commercial need, and identify ambiguities that require legal or technical clarification.
Verification
Vetting in this reconstructed approach: trace each proposed use to an express permission, restriction, or unresolved question; check consistency across definitions, use rights, and ownership language.

Scope & privacy. The CV identifies AI/ML and generative-AI contractual frameworks addressing training, data use, and ownership. No internal framework, counterparty, or clause is disclosed.

Approach

AI role
AI-assisted research organization, cross-jurisdiction comparison, and memo development. I separate information gathering from legal interpretation, then translate the analysis into operational recommendations.

Prompting & review sequence

Paraphrased intent · reconstructed process · no exact prompts

  1. Define the research map

    Prompting intent. Organize relevant jurisdictions, legal topics, and questions of scope and timing.

    My analysis & follow-through. I determine what is relevant to the organization and where the research needs to go deeper.

  2. Separate status from substance

    Prompting intent. Arrange developments by legal status, applicability, and operational significance.

    My analysis & follow-through. I check primary materials and correct conflation of enacted rules, proposals, and guidance.

  3. Translate into choices

    Prompting intent. Develop ways to connect the legal analysis with organization-wide actions and priorities.

04Contract processes

Make recurring decisions easier to handle.

Commercial legal work · Process design
Experience basis

Commercial work supported by CV; fallback and process emphasis described in Donny’s brief. AI use across this area confirmed by Donny; sequence below is a public reconstruction.

Problem
Repeated contract issues can consume attention without becoming easier to resolve.
Approach
Identify recurring questions; distinguish a preferred position from a possible fallback; record the rationale, business conditions, and points that require escalation; use those distinctions to inform templates and review processes.
AI role
AI-assisted comparison of recurring issues, exploration of fallback structures, and refinement of templates and process explanations. I decide what can be standardized and what must remain a judgment call.

Prompting & review sequence

Paraphrased intent · reconstructed process · no exact prompts

  1. Find the recurring decision

    Prompting intent. Group related review issues and distinguish variations that may matter.

    My analysis & follow-through. I decide whether the issues share a real rationale or merely similar wording.

  2. Develop a range

    Prompting intent. Compare a preferred position, possible fallbacks, and conditions for each.

    My analysis & follow-through. I assess the tradeoffs and define where a decision needs further review.

  3. Test exceptions

    Prompting intent. Explore scenarios in which a standard position would fail or need adaptation.

    My analysis & follow-through. I preserve necessary exceptions and avoid turning context-dependent judgment into a blanket rule.

  4. Make the process usable

    Prompting intent. Refine the organization and explanation of a template or review sequence.

    My analysis & follow-through. I check that the resulting guidance explains the reasoning and does not imply authority to approve a deviation.

Human judgment
Determine whether a fallback fits the transaction and who may approve a departure. A template cannot set its own risk tolerance.
Verification
Vetting in this reconstructed approach: test sample language against contrasting fictional scenarios; check that fallback terms remain internally consistent; identify the review owner and revision date.

Scope & privacy. This is a generalized process description, not a company playbook. It makes no claim about deployment, adoption, time saved, or measured business results.

Legal work & legal education

Problem
A commercial intention is not always captured by a clear grant of intellectual property rights.
Approach

AI role
AI-assisted issue mapping, analysis of licensing scope, and comparison of drafting alternatives. I use successive questions to uncover assumptions about ownership, permission, and intended use.

Prompting & review sequence

Paraphrased intent · reconstructed process · no exact prompts

  1. Identify the rights question

    Prompting intent. Organize the asset, intended uses, ownership assumptions, and required permissions.

    My analysis & follow-through. I distinguish facts supplied by a source from assumptions introduced by the analysis.

  2. Compare scope

    Prompting intent. Explore how changes in purpose, duration, territory, or modification rights affect a license.

    My analysis & follow-through. I select the distinctions that matter to the intended use.

  3. Probe ambiguity

    Prompting intent. Test competing readings of a grant or limitation using a generalized example.

    My analysis & follow-through. I examine the text and relevant authority before accepting an interpretation.

  4. Refine and verify

    Prompting intent. Help clarify the chosen explanation or drafting structure.

    My analysis & follow-through. I check authority, consistency, and whether the final language covers the intended uses without overreaching.

Human judgment
Interpret the grant, identify gaps in authority or scope, and choose language that reflects the parties’ intended relationship.
Verification
Vetting in this reconstructed approach: compare the grant against each intended use; verify authority and chain-of-rights assumptions; read the agreement as a whole for inconsistent limitations.

06Legal research & analysis

Make the reasoning traceable.

Legal employment & legal education
Experience basis

Research and executive-facing memoranda supported by CV; workflow generalized. AI use across this area confirmed by Donny; sequence below is a public reconstruction.

Problem
A decision-maker needs a clear answer without losing the assumptions and uncertainty on which it depends.
Approach
Frame the question; separate supplied facts from assumptions; identify relevant authority; test the analysis against contrary arguments; present the conclusion with practical implications and open issues.
AI role
AI-assisted issue framing, analytical organization, counterargument development, and revision. I treat generated explanations and citations as leads to evaluate, not authorities to rely on.

Prompting & review sequence

Paraphrased intent · reconstructed process · no exact prompts

  1. Frame the question

    Prompting intent. Break a broad issue into legal questions, factual dependencies, and research paths.

    My analysis & follow-through. I determine the scope and identify assumptions that must remain visible.

  2. Build competing analyses

    Prompting intent. Organize possible arguments and the authorities that would be needed to support them.

    My analysis & follow-through. I retrieve and read the sources instead of treating the generated account as verified research.

  3. Challenge the conclusion

    Prompting intent. Develop a contrary argument or identify facts that could change the result.

    My analysis & follow-through. I weigh the strongest objections and calibrate the conclusion to the available support.

  4. Write for the reader

    Prompting intent. Refine the structure and clarity of the explanation for its intended audience.

    My analysis & follow-through. I retain necessary qualifications and check every material proposition against its factual and legal basis.

Human judgment
Determine relevance, assess the strength of authority, weigh competing interpretations, and decide how much confidence the evidence supports.
Verification
Vetting in this reconstructed approach: open and read cited authorities; verify quotations and propositions; check subsequent treatment where relevant; trace conclusions back to the facts and sources.

Scope & privacy. The CV supports internal legal briefs, executive-facing memoranda, and legal research. Donny confirms AI use across this area; no assignment, source transcript, or exact prompt is reproduced.

07Mediation

Listen for the interests beneath the positions.

Cardozo Mediation Clinic · 2025–2026
Experience basis

Mediation experience supported by CV; process reconstructed without party information. AI use across this area confirmed by Donny; sequence below is a public reconstruction.

Problem
Parties may arrive with incompatible positions and different ideas of what a workable resolution requires.
Approach
Prepare through intake; listen to each account; clarify interests and constraints; help parties explore options through structured negotiation.
AI role
AI-assisted analysis of interests, exploration of question structures, and reflection on alternative approaches. AI supports my thinking about the process; it does not establish what a participant thinks or choose a resolution.

Prompting & review sequence

Paraphrased intent · reconstructed process · no exact prompts

  1. Distinguish positions and interests

    Prompting intent. Explore different explanations for a generalized conflict without assuming any is true.

    My analysis & follow-through. I treat these as hypotheses to test through listening, not findings about the people involved.

  2. Develop questions

    Prompting intent. Compare neutral, open-ended ways to clarify needs and constraints.

    My analysis & follow-through. I assess tone, neutrality, and whether a question could lead or mischaracterize someone.

  3. Explore possible paths

    Prompting intent. Consider ways to structure a discussion or compare potential resolution options.

    My analysis & follow-through. I judge suitability in context; participant consent and agency remain central.

  4. Reflect and refine

    Prompting intent. Examine alternative approaches and assumptions in a generalized account of the process.

    My analysis & follow-through. I separate useful reflection from unsupported conclusions and return to what participants actually communicate.

Human judgment
Attend to consent, neutrality, communication, and the parties’ ability to make their own decisions. The parties retain ownership of any resolution.
Verification
Vetting in this reconstructed approach: ask participants to confirm that their interests and any proposed terms are accurately understood; check clarity and practical feasibility without promising an outcome.

Scope & privacy. The CV records court-annexed and community mediation, intake, preparation, and structured negotiation. No dispute, participant, settlement term, or outcome is described.

03 / Working with AI

An exchange, followed by judgment.

01

Frame the question.

I begin with the objective, relevant context, constraints, and the kind of help needed. A useful exchange starts by making the problem more precise.

02

Sequence the work.

I separate issue mapping, comparison, challenge, and refinement. Each stage informs the next; a first response is a starting point for analysis.

03

Interrogate the output.

I look for assumptions, omissions, competing interpretations, and conclusions that move beyond their support. I change the question when the framing is wrong.

04

Incorporate selectively.

I compare the response with the task and my own analysis, retain useful structure or alternatives, and revise or discard what does not fit.

05

Vet against evidence.

I return to sources, facts, actual language, and practical constraints. Coherent prose is not proof; unresolved questions need to remain visible.

06

Own the result.

I remain responsible for interpretation, proportionality, escalation, and communication. Applicable rules and information boundaries govern how assistance can be used.
